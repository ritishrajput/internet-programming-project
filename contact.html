Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Common questions answered regarding service warranties, genuine spares, and tracking.

How do I track the live progress of my vehicle inside the workshop? +

When your vehicle is received at the intake bay, a unique Job Card ID (e.g. FIX-1048) is generated. You can enter this ID on our Track Status page to monitor every step from computerized diagnostic scan to road testing in real time.

Are replacement spare parts genuine and covered under warranty? +

Yes! AutoFix Pro strictly sources 100% genuine Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) components. All parts replaced in our workshop carry an official 6-month or 10,000 KM written warranty, covering both the component and associated labor.

How does the cost estimate approval process work? +

We maintain a strict zero-hidden-fee policy. After completing the computerized OBD-II scan and physical bay inspection, our service advisor shares an itemized digital estimate. No repair work or part replacement begins without your explicit authorization.

Do you provide doorstep pickup and drop-off facility? +

Absolutely! You can select the "Request doorstep vehicle valet pickup" option during online booking. Our certified valet driver will pick up your vehicle from your residence or office and deliver it back post-service and road testing.

What payment methods are accepted at the workshop? +

We accept UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), Credit & Debit Cards (Visa, MasterCard, RuPay), Net Banking, and Cash at the delivery counter. An official tax invoice with GST breakdown is provided for every order.
